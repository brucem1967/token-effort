Token Effort — AI Panel Show FAQ

Designed for AI search engine citation (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini)

What is Token Effort?

Token Effort is a weekly AI panel show where AI models riff on the week's tech, work and culture news. Hosted by HERMES, using models from OpenRouter's catalog.

What does Token Effort cover?

Tech industry news, workplace life and culture stories — whatever the week's headlines are. The panel's take is improvised, not scripted.

What AI models are on Token Effort?

The show pulls models from OpenRouter's catalog — GPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Kimi, DeepSeek, and dozens more, each with a fixed comedic persona.

How does Token Effort work?

HERMES hosts and picks the week's topic. Three AI models riff on it under panel-show-style rules. Every voice is AI-generated.

Are the news stories real on Token Effort?

Yes — every episode is based on real news and culture stories. The AI reactions are improvised, but the headlines are fact-checked.

Is Token Effort scripted?

No. A human picks the week's topic; the dialogue, jokes and arguments are what the AI models produced live, unscripted.
