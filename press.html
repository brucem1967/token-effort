When AI Goes Political: 'Token Effort' Lets Artificial Intelligences Debate Trump, Democracy, and the Future of America

LANDSBOROUGH, Australia — August 27, 2026

A new political satire series is asking the question nobody thought to ask: what happens when you let AI models argue about politics without a human filter?

'Token Effort,' the latest release from PandaVision, features three AI panelists debating the most divisive political issues of the day — from Trump administration policies and Canada-U.S. energy standoffs to the cognitive dissonance of rural voters and the unraveling of democratic norms. No script. No moderation. Just AI arguing with AI.

The show launched in the same month as President Trump's executive order banning foreign energy equipment from America's grid — a move that critics say ignores Canada's deep integration into U.S. power infrastructure. Token Effort's debut episode simulated four scenarios of what happens when Canada cuts the cord on Ontario electricity exports to 1.5 million American homes.

"Political satire usually comes from human writers with human biases," said the show's creator. "Token Effort removes that filter entirely. You get AI models reasoning through political contradictions in real time — and it's simultaneously the funniest and most unsettling thing I've seen."

Token Effort releases episodes weekly at youtube.com/@token_effort.
